01 / The sensing principle

A photon’s journey.

The optical prototype illuminates tissue at 940 nm. A photodetector measures the transmitted signal, which is conditioned as a voltage for model calibration.

01 / EMIT940 nm NIR source

An infrared emitter directs light through the measurement site.

02 / MEASUREPhotodetection

Transmitted intensity depends on tissue absorption and scattering.

03 / CONDITIONAnalog signal

Amplification and filtering yield the measurable output voltage.

06 / Reproducible training protocol

Follow the weights.
Watch the prediction.

One deterministic swarm recipe regenerates every neural-weight vector in this release. A separate constrained calibration maps the neural response to the manuscript's fitted numerical summaries. Inspect both stages below.

02 / OPTIMIZE25 particles × 70 updates

Standardized, seeded PSO fits the 17-weight network.

03 / CALIBRATE30 kernel anchors

Summary-constrained Gaussian discrepancy correction.

Predict from inputs; score fitted observations separately.
